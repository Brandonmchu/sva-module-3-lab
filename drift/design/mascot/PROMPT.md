## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: preliminary mascot character exploration sheet for a friendly group travel planning app; brainstorming preview, pending the product manager's final character notes.

Primary request: Design one original, very cute RED PANDA character shown in four consistent emotional states as the group's trip becomes more planned, ending in relaxed vacation mode.

Subject: A charming compact red panda with warm rusty-red fur, cream cheek markings and muzzle, dark chocolate paws and little legs, rounded ears, an exceptionally fluffy ringed tail, soft rounded body, expressive dark eyes. Clearly a red panda, with the same facial markings, body proportions, and tail design in every pose. Appealing to adult friend groups, welcoming and playful without looking like a preschool toy.

Style/medium: Polished soft 3D character concept, matte tactile surfaces, gently sculpted simplified fur, expressive silhouette, premium travel-app illustration, readable at small UI sizes.

Composition/framing: A beautifully arranged landscape concept sheet with four equal standalone full-body character poses in a clean 2 by 2 grid. Spacious pale sky-blue studio background, soft minimal grounding shadows, no UI frames, no explanatory copy, no lettering, no watermarks. Keep all tails and ears inside the image. Same apparent character scale in each cell.

Four poses in reading order: top left, beginning the plan, the panda sits upright holding a small paper travel checklist with a curious, slightly frazzled but hopeful expression, one ear a bit tilted; top right, making progress, the panda is upright and smiling warmly while confidently ticking his small checklist; bottom left, plans almost ready, the panda sits comfortably nestled against his own tail with a calm happy smile and relaxed shoulders; bottom right, vacation mode, the panda wears a tiny straw sunhat and sunglasses, reclining blissfully against his fluffy tail with a clear happy smile and paws resting behind his head.

Lighting/mood: Bright, airy, gentle daylight, warm welcoming charm.

Color palette: Rusty red, warm cream, dark brown; the airy sky-blue setting and tiny restrained sunny-yellow details fit a bright modern travel app.

Constraints: This is one mascot with four poses, not four different designs. Do not add brand names. No crying, fear, guilt, alarm symbols, emergency imagery, phones, or extra characters. The change in happiness must come through clear face and body language.

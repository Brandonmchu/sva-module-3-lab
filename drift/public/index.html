How it works

From "we should go!" to booked in three steps.

  1. Start the trip

    Pick the destination, dates and a budget per person. Takes about a minute.

  2. Everyone adds in

    Each friend shares when they're free, what they can spend and what they're into.

  3. Decide together

    Vote on ideas, see what's agreed and who's booking it. No one has to be the debt collector.

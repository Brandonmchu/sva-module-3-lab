# Lorette (Ember team) vision

## Customer
Bachelorette groups planning a weekend away: the maid of honour or bride-to-be organising friends who all need to say yes.

## Promise
Plan the weekend together, and make the planning feel like part of the party.

## Look
Glam: blush, raspberry, champagne gold, serif headings, polaroid-style keepsakes.

## Voice
Fun, flirty and celebration-first.

- Playful and confident, never stiff or corporate.
- A little sexy: teasing, glamorous, cheeky. Not crude.
- Everything is a reason to celebrate: dates, votes, a confirmed plan.
- Short, punchy lines. Exclamation marks sparingly.
- Say "the squad", "the bride", "the weekend". Avoid "travelers" and "users".

Examples:
- "Lock it in" instead of "Confirm plan"
- "The squad" instead of "Travelers"
- "Who's in?" instead of "Respond to the plan"

## Roles
- Designer: look and feel (`public/styles.css`).
- Product marketer: landing page, onboarding and copy (`public/index.html`).
- Product manager: features and behavior (`public/app.js`).

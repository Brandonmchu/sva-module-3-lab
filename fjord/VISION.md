# Vision

## Product promise

Fjord helps a group of friends turn a spoken idea for a weekend away into a few appealing, practical trip plans everyone can shape together.

## The experience

A traveler starts by speaking naturally: where they might go, when they can travel, their group size, per-person budget, and the places or experiences they hope to include. They can also type or correct any detail. Fjord turns the conversation into a short, visible trip brief and asks focused follow-up questions when essential details are missing or unclear.

Once the traveler reviews the brief, Fjord prepares several distinct route options, such as:

- **Best fit:** balances the group's budget, timing, and shared interests.
- **Lower cost:** favors affordable activities and practical travel between stops.
- **More to explore:** includes more of the requested sights and experiences, with costs called out clearly.

Each option is a draft. The traveler can compare it with the others, choose one, and edit destinations, stops, dates, timing, or estimated costs before sharing it with the group. Fjord keeps the group in control of the final plan.

## The visual plan

Fjord presents the selected route as a clear, attractive HTML itinerary plan that works on desktop and phone. It shows the trip dates, route order, daily schedule, travel transitions, and estimated per-person costs. A simple route diagram or map-style view helps travelers understand how the stops connect. The itinerary remains readable and useful when a map is unavailable.

Fjord automatically finds suitable photos for destinations and suggested places and places them beside the relevant itinerary sections. Photos should help the group picture the experience, load efficiently, and include source attribution when required. Travelers can replace or remove a photo. Images are illustrative; they do not imply that availability, opening hours, prices, or reservations have been verified.

## Product principles

- **Start with what the group said.** Keep the spoken request visible as editable trip details.
- **Offer meaningful choices.** Present a small set of routes with clear differences and trade-offs.
- **Make the plan editable.** AI suggestions are a starting point, and travelers can revise them directly.
- **Be clear about uncertainty.** Distinguish estimates and suggestions from verified travel information.
- **Keep the group involved.** Make it easy to share the draft and incorporate each traveler's budget, availability, and interests.
- **Design for a weekend away.** Keep the experience friendly, visual, and straightforward to use on a phone.

## First release direction

Begin with voice capture, editable requirement extraction, a small set of generated itinerary drafts, and a responsive HTML plan with route visualization and relevant photos. Keep booking and payment outside the first release. Save the chosen draft into Fjord's existing trip and activity model so the group can continue planning together.

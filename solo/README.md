# Solo exercise: unblock your pull request

Your folder is `solo/<your-github-handle>/`. Your branch is `solo-<your-github-handle>`.

## The riddle

1. On your branch, put your company name in the headline of `index.html`. Push. Open a pull request into `main`.
2. GitHub will say that your branch has conflicts. Find out why, with your agent.
3. Get the pull request merged. The headline on `main` must show your company name AND the other change.

Done: your pull request says "Merged", and your tile on the scoreboard is green.

## Bonus riddle

The Buy button on `checkout.html` vanished a few commits ago. Bring it back, and keep everything that was added after it.

## Rules

Your agent reads `AGENTS.md` in your folder. Work only in your own folder. Never push to `main` directly.
